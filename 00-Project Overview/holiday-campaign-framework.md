# Holiday Campaign Framework

Holiday campaigns are among the highest-intent, highest-revenue moments of the year. This framework
documents the major holidays we plan for and, for each, the **purpose, theme, typical products,
promotion ideas, design direction, customer psychology, and business objective**. It is a planning
library: the Campaign Manager selects the occasion and uses the row below as the starting brief.

The calendar is Australian-market first (all brands ship Australia-wide). Dates that move each year
(e.g. Easter, EOFY sales, Mother's/Father's Day) are confirmed against the current year in the
[Content Calendar](content-calendar.md).

## Planning principles for holidays

- **Plan early.** Holiday sends should be briefed weeks ahead, not in the final days.
- **Respect the mood.** Some holidays are celebratory and gift-led; others (e.g. EOFY) are practical and
  value-led. The tone must match.
- **Verify offers first.** Any coupon or discount must be confirmed created and active before send
  (see [Campaign Standards](Campaign-Standards.md)).
- **Sequence, don't spam.** For big events (Black Friday / Cyber Monday), plan a short sequence (tease →
  launch → last chance) rather than one email.

## Holiday library

### Christmas
- **Purpose:** capture peak gifting and end-of-year spend.
- **Theme:** warmth, giving, "treat someone you care about", festive.
- **Typical products:** gift-friendly and hero products, bundles, gift-ready price points.
- **Promotion ideas:** gift guides, bundle offers, free/expedited shipping cut-offs, last-order dates.
- **Design direction:** festive but on-brand; warm imagery; clear shipping-deadline messaging.
- **Customer psychology:** generosity, time pressure, desire to get it right for others.
- **Business objective:** maximise Q4 revenue and average order value.

### New Year
- **Purpose:** capture "fresh start" motivation.
- **Theme:** new beginnings, goals, improvement, a better year ahead.
- **Typical products:** items that support new routines, independence, safety, organisation.
- **Promotion ideas:** new-year offer, "start the year right" bundles.
- **Design direction:** clean, optimistic, forward-looking.
- **Customer psychology:** motivation, optimism, self- and family-improvement.
- **Business objective:** re-activate customers after the holiday lull.

### Easter
- **Purpose:** long-weekend seasonal moment and autumn transition.
- **Theme:** family, home, getting out and about over the break.
- **Typical products:** family- and home-oriented range; outing/mobility items.
- **Promotion ideas:** long-weekend sale, family-focused bundles.
- **Design direction:** light, fresh, family-warm.
- **Customer psychology:** family time, relaxed spending over a break.
- **Business objective:** lift sales across a traditionally quieter period.

### Black Friday
- **Purpose:** the single biggest deal moment of the year.
- **Theme:** genuine, unmissable savings; urgency.
- **Typical products:** best-sellers and high-demand hero products.
- **Promotion ideas:** headline storewide offer, doorbusters, tiered discounts; tease → launch → last chance sequence.
- **Design direction:** bold, high-contrast, unmistakably a sale; prominent code and deadline.
- **Customer psychology:** deal-seeking, fear of missing out, competitive urgency.
- **Business objective:** maximise volume and new-customer acquisition.

### Cyber Monday
- **Purpose:** extend and close the Black Friday weekend.
- **Theme:** "last chance", online-only savings.
- **Typical products:** carry-over best-sellers, online exclusives.
- **Promotion ideas:** final-hours messaging, extended or stepped-down offer.
- **Design direction:** urgent, countdown-led, consistent with Black Friday creative.
- **Customer psychology:** urgency, closing the deal, regret-avoidance.
- **Business objective:** capture procrastinators and lift weekend total.

### EOFY (End of Financial Year)
- **Purpose:** capitalise on the strong Australian June "tax-time / EOFY sale" mindset.
- **Theme:** value, practicality, "get it sorted before 30 June", possible business/tax angle.
- **Typical products:** practical, higher-value and equipment items; anything justifiable as a
  before-EOFY purchase.
- **Promotion ideas:** EOFY sale, clearance, bundle value.
- **Design direction:** confident, value-forward, professional.
- **Customer psychology:** practicality, deadline, sense of a smart/timely purchase.
- **Business objective:** drive a strong end-of-June revenue peak.

### Australia Day
- **Purpose:** national moment with an Australian-owned, local angle.
- **Theme:** proudly Australian, local, community.
- **Typical products:** broad range; lean on "Australian owned / Australia-wide" trust signals.
- **Promotion ideas:** Australia Day sale, local-pride messaging.
- **Design direction:** clean, confident, tasteful national cues (avoid cliché).
- **Customer psychology:** local pride, trust in a local supplier.
- **Business objective:** reinforce the local-supplier positioning and lift January sales.

### Mother's Day
- **Purpose:** gifting moment focused on mothers and carers.
- **Theme:** care, appreciation, comfort, thoughtfulness.
- **Typical products:** comfort, wellbeing, and thoughtful-gift items.
- **Promotion ideas:** gift guide "for Mum", gift bundles.
- **Design direction:** warm, soft, appreciative.
- **Customer psychology:** gratitude, care for a loved one.
- **Business objective:** capture seasonal gifting spend.

### Father's Day
- **Purpose:** gifting moment focused on fathers and carers.
- **Theme:** practicality, reliability, appreciation.
- **Typical products:** practical, useful, "something he'll actually use" items.
- **Promotion ideas:** gift guide "for Dad", value bundles.
- **Design direction:** clean, practical, confident.
- **Customer psychology:** appreciation, wanting a useful and valued gift.
- **Business objective:** capture seasonal gifting spend.

### Labour Day
- **Purpose:** long-weekend sale moment (date varies by state).
- **Theme:** long weekend, a well-earned break/deal.
- **Typical products:** broad range; best-sellers.
- **Promotion ideas:** long-weekend sale.
- **Design direction:** simple, upbeat, sale-led.
- **Customer psychology:** relaxed, deal-friendly long-weekend mood.
- **Business objective:** lift sales over the long weekend.

### Boxing Day
- **Purpose:** the major post-Christmas sale event.
- **Theme:** big clearance, "treat yourself now".
- **Typical products:** clearance, overstock, best-sellers.
- **Promotion ideas:** Boxing Day sale, deep clearance, stepped discounts.
- **Design direction:** bold, sale-led, high urgency.
- **Customer psychology:** self-gifting after the giving season, bargain-hunting.
- **Business objective:** clear stock and capture strong post-Christmas demand.

## Using this framework

Pick the occasion, copy its row into a campaign brief (see
[Campaign Planning Framework](campaign-planning-framework.md)), then adapt it to the specific brand and
current-year dates. Brand-specific rules (voice, colours, coupon conventions) always come from the
[Brand documentation](../03-Brands/).
