# Executive Vision

> **Read this first.** This section explains *why* the Email Campaign System exists and where
> we want it to go. It is written for people, not machines. The detailed "how" lives in the later
> chapters and in the AI operating manual (`CLAUDE.md`), which is a separate document.

## Our vision

To make every scheduled marketing email we send feel like it came from one confident, well-run
brand family — professionally designed, on-message, and produced quickly enough that the team can
focus on strategy instead of rebuilding the same email from scratch each week.

We are building a **repeatable campaign engine**, not a collection of one-off emails. Over time this
engine should let us launch a high-quality campaign for any brand, for any occasion, in a fraction of
the time it takes today, while raising quality rather than lowering it.

## Why this project exists

Marketing email is one of the most cost-effective channels we own. It reaches customers who already
know us, it is measurable, and it drives repeat revenue. Yet its quality has depended too much on who
happened to build a given send and how much time they had. The result is uneven: strong one week,
rushed the next.

This project turns campaign production into a **documented, repeatable system** so that quality and
consistency come from the process, not from heroics. It captures how we plan, design, review, and ship
campaigns so that any team member — or an assisting tool — can produce an on-brand result predictably.

## Why documentation matters

- **It makes knowledge shared, not personal.** When the process lives only in one person's head, the
  business is exposed the moment that person is unavailable. Written standards make the team resilient.
- **It makes quality repeatable.** A documented standard can be reviewed, improved, and held to. An
  undocumented habit cannot.
- **It makes onboarding fast.** New team members (and new brands) can come up to speed from the
  documents instead of by trial and error.
- **It is the plan we build from.** In line with our "plan before building" principle, this document is
  approved first and then guides implementation.

## Why consistency matters

Consistency is not sameness. Each brand keeps its own voice and look. Consistency means a customer
always recognises the brand, always finds the email easy to read, and always trusts what they see —
whether it arrives in July or December, on a phone or a laptop. Consistent structure also compounds:
every reusable component we standardise makes the *next* campaign cheaper and better.

## The three-horizon view

- **Now** — a reliable, documented weekly and monthly process across all brands, produced faster with
  fewer errors.
- **Next** — a richer campaign toolkit: a holiday and seasonal library, brand templates, and assisted
  review, so planning is calendar-led rather than reactive.
- **Later** — a measured, semi-automated system: dynamic product selection, analytics-informed
  decisions, and testing built into the workflow. These are captured in the
  [Future Roadmap](../05-Future/roadmap.md) and remain proposals until approved.
