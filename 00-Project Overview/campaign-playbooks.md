# Campaign Playbooks

_Part of the BRD modular source. Human-first: what the playbooks are and how the team uses them. The
playbooks themselves live in [`Playbooks/`](../Playbooks/)._

## What a playbook is

A Campaign Playbook is the **strategy brief for one campaign type**. It captures how that type should
think, look, read and be measured, so anyone producing that kind of send starts from shared, proven
direction instead of reinventing it each time. Playbooks are living documents: each completed campaign
should feed a lesson back in.

## The twelve sections (every playbook)

1. **Purpose** — what this type is for.
2. **Business Goal** — the commercial outcome it serves.
3. **Customer Psychology** — the mindset and motivations it speaks to.
4. **Copywriting Style** — voice, length, do/don't (including the no-dash-interruption rule).
5. **Design Direction** — look, hierarchy, structure.
6. **Hero Strategy** — how the top of the email earns attention.
7. **CTA Strategy** — the primary action and how to frame it.
8. **Product Strategy** — how products are chosen, verified and presented.
9. **KPIs** — how success is measured, and the one primary metric.
10. **Bruce Feedback** — standing feedback that must be honoured.
11. **Common Mistakes** — the traps to avoid.
12. **Lessons Learned** — accumulated, campaign-tested insight.

## How playbooks fit the workflow

The playbook is step 4 of the [Execution Order](execution-order.md): read `CLAUDE.md` and the BRD, read
the brand sources, then read the playbook for the type before building. The playbook explains *why*; the
matching generate prompt in `07-Prompt Library/` explains *how*.
