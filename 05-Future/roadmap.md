# Future Enhancements & Roadmap

This roadmap captures where we want to take the system. Everything here is a **proposal** and a
direction of travel, not a commitment — items move into scope only after review and approval by the
stakeholders (see [Scope](../00-Project%20Overview/scope.md) and
[Stakeholders](../00-Project%20Overview/stakeholders.md)). Sequencing follows the three-horizon view in
the [Executive Vision](../00-Project%20Overview/executive-vision.md): stabilise now, enrich next,
automate later.

## Horizon 1 — Enrich the toolkit (next)

- **Holiday Campaign Library.** Turn the [Holiday Framework](../00-Project%20Overview/holiday-campaign-framework.md)
  into ready-to-brief, reusable campaign kits per occasion — faster, more consistent seasonal sends.
- **Brand Templates.** Pre-built, on-brand starting templates per brand so each send begins from an
  approved base instead of a blank page. Reduces production time and drift.
- **AI Review System.** Assisted pre-QA that checks a draft against standards (links, images, stock,
  accessibility, brand rules) and flags issues before human review. Augments, never replaces, the human
  approver.

## Horizon 2 — Reduce manual effort (later)

- **Automatic Product Selection.** Pull in-stock, on-theme products directly from the commerce platform
  so grids are built and stock-checked automatically, removing a major manual step and a common error
  source.
- **Dynamic Hero Banner Generation.** Assisted creation of on-brand hero visuals from a controlled
  prompt library, with mandatory human review for accuracy and brand fit before use.

## Horizon 3 — Measure and optimise (later)

- **Campaign Analytics.** A consolidated view of the [Success Metrics](../00-Project%20Overview/success-metrics.md)
  across brands and campaigns, so decisions are data-led.
- **A/B Testing Framework.** A repeatable way to test subject lines, offers, creative, and layout, and
  fold the winners back into templates and standards.
- **Customer Journey Mapping.** Understand how scheduled campaigns fit alongside the separate automated
  flows, so the overall customer experience is coherent (coordination only — flows remain out of scope
  here).

## How items graduate from the roadmap

1. A proposal is raised here with its rationale and expected benefit.
2. Stakeholders review it against current priorities and capacity.
3. If approved, it is moved into scope, documented in the relevant section, and recorded in the
   [Decision Log](../09-Architecture%20Decisions/Decision-Log.md).
4. Until then, it stays a proposal and is **not** implemented (consistent with the "plan before building"
   principle).
