## Campaign concept (fresh for W29)

Mid-winter is peak season for slips, falls and reduced confidence at home. Rather than repeat W28's
broad "Winter Mobility Event" discount framing, W29 is a focused **home-safety + independence** edit:
help customers stay **safe, steady and independent through the coldest weeks** — safer bathrooms, surer
footing, easier home access, and reliable ways to get out and about.

- **Eyebrow:** Winter Safety Edit
- **Headline:** *Stay Safe, Steady And Independent This Winter*
- **Support (one concise line, no dashes):** A trusted range to keep everyday life easier at home, chosen
  and backed by our friendly Australian team.
- **First-glance hierarchy (CLAUDE.md §6.3):** Brand logo → hero hook (eyebrow + headline + one support
  line + primary CTA) → quick category pills → products. Intro kept to a single short line (W28's
  three-paragraph intro was too long).
